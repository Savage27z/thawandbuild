# Lofi: Thaw & Build - art bible
Look: Moebius (fine ink linework + hatching, flat pastel color fields, vast clean sky, tiny hero vs monumental land) x Roger Dean (floating islands, sweeping organic rock/ice arches, glowing sunset sky, impossible formations).
Concept art (the quality bar, compare screenshots against these): art/concept-world.png, art/concept-characters.png, art/concept-combat.png.
Palette: see PALETTE in src/style.js. Sky pale cyan to peach gradient. Ice pale blue with darker blue-lilac shadows. Rock warm terracotta with plum shadows. Clean water turquoise, sludge near-black purple with magenta glow cracks. Ink outlines dark indigo. Shading: 3-4 band toon, hatching in shadows if feasible.
Hero: Lofi, white fur, pale-blue face, blue scarf that trails in motion, brown backpack. Enemies: sludge imps (small, yellow eyes), sludge golem (purple cracks), boss Grime Titan (red cracks, pipes/barrels on its back).
Stack: three + vite, run 'npx vite --host --port 5173'. No external assets; everything procedural. Keep 60fps on a laptop.
